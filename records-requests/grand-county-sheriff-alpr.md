# GRAMA request: Grand County Sheriff's Office, license plate readers

Status: drafted, not filed. File it through Grand County's records request page
(https://www.grandcountyutah.net/592/Open-Records-Request-GRAMA) or the county's GRAMA form,
addressed to the Sheriff's Office. Fill in the bracketed parts first. Utah law requires the
requester's name, mailing address and a phone number if available.

---

[Date]

Records Officer
Grand County Sheriff's Office
Moab, Utah

Re: Records request under the Government Records Access and Management Act (Utah Code 63G-2)

From: [Full name], [mailing address], [phone], [email]

I request copies of the following records, in electronic form where they exist:

1. Every contract, quote, purchase order and invoice for any automatic license plate reader
   system or license plate data service the Sheriff's Office has used or paid for since
   January 1, 2023, whatever the vendor.
2. The office's current written policy on automatic license plate readers, and any earlier
   version in effect since January 1, 2023.
3. Any audit, review, inspection or compliance report on the office's use of license plate
   readers or license plate data completed since January 1, 2025, by the office or by any
   outside body, together with the office's written response.
4. Any agreement or memorandum of understanding under which the office shares license plate
   data with, or receives it from, another agency, including the Utah Department of Public Safety.
5. For January 1, 2025 to the date of this request: the number of license plate data searches
   run by the office each month, and the search audit log showing the date of each search and
   the case number or reason entered for it. Officer names may be redacted if the office
   considers them protected.

Expedited response. I ask for a response within five business days. The Sheriff is on the
November 3, 2026 ballot, license plate readers were a question at the September 28 candidate
forum, and I will publish these records for voters before the election at no charge. A prompt
response benefits the public rather than me personally.

Fees. I ask that fees be waived because releasing these records primarily benefits the public.
If the waiver is denied, please send a fee estimate before doing any work that would cost more
than $25.

If any record or part of a record is withheld, please cite the specific provision relied on,
release the rest, and tell me how to appeal.

Thank you,

[Full name]
